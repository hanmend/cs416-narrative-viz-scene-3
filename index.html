2017 Automobile Fuel Efficiency

Now, we can see the breakdown by engine fuel type.

Gasoline cars are the most common, but they are also the least fuel efficient. The data points in the bottom left of the chart indicate that gasoline cars can have very low fuel efficiency, especially when compared to electric cars.

Electric cars are the most fuel efficient, with many models achieving over 100 miles per gallon equivalent (MPGe).

Diesel cars fall somewhere in between, with better fuel efficiency than gasoline but not as high as electric vehicles.
