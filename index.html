2017 Automobile Fuel Efficiency

Here, we can focus on one car make (Chevrolet). In this dataset, Chevrolet has cars using all three fuel types.
The Chevrolet cars follow the same general pattern and trend which we saw in the whole dataset, with gasoline usually sitting lower, diesel in the middle, and electric as a huge outlier with vastly higher efficiency.
